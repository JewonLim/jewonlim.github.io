# Jewon Lim

I am an M.S. student in the Department of Industrial & Management Engineering at **Incheon National University**, where I conduct research in the **AI & Optimization Lab** under the supervision of Prof. Jae-Gon Kim.

My research focuses on **neural combinatorial optimization (NCO)** and **reinforcement learning (RL)** for complex scheduling and combinatorial optimization problems. I am particularly interested in learning-based optimization methods that are scalable, generalizable, and applicable to real-world manufacturing environments.

## Research Interests

- Neural combinatorial optimization and reinforcement learning
- Production scheduling and manufacturing optimization
- Attention-based neural models and graph neural networks
- Generalization and scalable learning-based optimization
